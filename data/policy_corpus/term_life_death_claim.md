# Term Life Death Claim — Process and Documents

## How to report a death claim

The nominee, or a legal heir if no nominee is registered, should inform us of the death as soon as possible through support, quoting the policy number, the insured person's name, and the date and cause of death. A claim ID is issued, which can be used to track the death claim status at any time.

## Documents required for a death claim

For a term life death claim, submit the claim form filled by the nominee, the death certificate issued by the local authority, the policy document or a copy, the nominee's photo ID and address proof, and a cancelled cheque or bank passbook copy. For death due to illness, add the medical records and hospital death summary. For accidental or unnatural death, add the FIR, post-mortem report and police final report.

## How long death claim settlement takes

A death claim is decided within 30 days of receiving all required documents. If the claim needs further investigation, for example when death happens within the first three years of the policy, the claim is decided within 90 days of intimation. The claim amount is paid by bank transfer to the nominee's account.

## Why a death claim may be rejected

A death claim may be rejected if the policy had lapsed on the date of death because the premium was not paid by the end of the 30-day grace period, if death was due to suicide within 12 months of the policy start (only premiums paid are refunded), or if material facts were not disclosed at proposal. A rejected claim can be appealed in writing to the Grievance Redressal Officer within 15 days, quoting the claim number.
