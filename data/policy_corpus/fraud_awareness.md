# Fraud Awareness and Safety Tips

## Never share your OTP

We will never ask for your OTP, password, PIN, card CVV or full bank details over a phone call, SMS, email or chat. An OTP is only for you to enter yourself during verification. If anyone asks for your OTP, even someone claiming to be from our company, do not share it and report the call to support.

## Common insurance and loan frauds

Watch out for callers offering a bonus, refund or higher claim amount in exchange for a processing fee; messages urging you to surrender or switch your policy immediately; fake links to pay premiums; and instant loan offers that ask for an upfront fee. We never ask for any fee to release a claim, bonus or loan.

## Pay only through official channels

Pay premiums and EMIs only through your own account, the official app, or a payment link sent to your registered mobile number or email. Always check that the payment receipt shows your own policy or loan number. Never pay premiums or EMIs into a personal bank account or wallet shared by a caller.

## What to do if you suspect fraud

If you shared an OTP or paid someone by mistake, contact support immediately to secure your account and raise a complaint; you will receive a ticket ID. Also inform your bank and report the incident to the cyber crime helpline or your local police.
