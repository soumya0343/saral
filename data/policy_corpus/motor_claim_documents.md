# Motor Claim — Documents and Survey

## How to report a motor claim

Inform us as soon as possible after an accident, theft or damage, and before starting any repairs, through support or your account. You receive a claim ID to track your motor claim. For theft and major accidents, file an FIR at the nearest police station and share a copy with us.

## Documents for a motor own-damage claim

For an own-damage claim, submit the filled claim form, a copy of the registration certificate (RC), the driving licence of the person driving at the time, the policy copy, photos of the damage, the garage's repair estimate, and the FIR where required. For a reimbursement claim, also add the final repair invoice, payment receipt and a cancelled cheque.

## Vehicle survey and repair

Before repairs, a surveyor appointed by us inspects the vehicle, usually within 48 hours of claim intimation, to assess the damage and approve the repair estimate. At a network garage, the approved amount is settled directly with the garage; at other garages, you pay and claim reimbursement. You pay the compulsory deductible, and depreciation on parts unless you have the zero-depreciation add-on.

## Documents for a vehicle theft claim

For a theft claim, you also need the FIR, the police final report stating that the vehicle could not be traced, all original keys, the original RC, and signed transfer forms so that ownership can be moved to the insurer. Theft claims are settled after the police final report is received.
