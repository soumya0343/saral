# Loan EMI — Frequently Asked Questions

Your EMI date is fixed at disbursal and can be changed once per loan tenure by raising a request.
An EMI bounce due to insufficient funds incurs a bounce charge plus penal interest on the overdue
amount.

You can download your loan statement and amortization schedule from your account. A no-objection
certificate (NOC) is issued within 7 days of full repayment.
