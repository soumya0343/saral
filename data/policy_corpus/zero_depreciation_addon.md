# Zero-Depreciation Add-On

## What zero depreciation means

In a normal motor own-damage claim, depreciation is deducted from the cost of replaced parts based on the vehicle's age and the part's material; rubber, nylon and plastic parts, for example, attract 50% depreciation. With the zero-depreciation add-on, also called bumper-to-bumper cover, the full cost of replaced parts is paid without deducting depreciation.

## What the zero-depreciation add-on does not cover

The zero-depreciation add-on does not waive the compulsory deductible. It does not cover mechanical breakdown, wear and tear, tyres unless damaged in an accident along with the vehicle, or consumables such as engine oil, unless you have separate add-ons for them. It does not apply to third-party claims.

## Zero-depreciation limits and eligibility

The zero-depreciation add-on is available for private cars up to 5 years old and allows up to 2 claims per policy year; claims beyond that are settled with normal depreciation. It is bought for an extra premium when you buy or renew a comprehensive or own-damage policy, and it cannot be added in the middle of the policy year.
