# Cashless Claims

## What a cashless claim is

A cashless claim lets you get hospital treatment without paying the covered amount from your own pocket. The insurer settles the approved bill directly with the hospital. Cashless treatment is available only at network hospitals, which are hospitals that have a tie-up with us. If you are admitted to a non-network hospital, you pay the bill yourself and file a reimbursement claim afterwards.

## How to get cashless approval

For cashless treatment, show your health card and a photo ID at the hospital's insurance desk. The hospital sends a pre-authorization request to us, and the request is decided within 4 hours. For a planned admission, ask the hospital to send the request at least 48 hours before admission. For an emergency admission, the request should reach us within 24 hours of admission.

If cashless pre-authorization is approved, the approved amount is paid directly to the hospital at discharge after a final bill review. If pre-authorization is denied or only partly approved, you can still take the treatment and later file a reimbursement claim with full documents. A cashless denial is not the same as a claim rejection.

## What you still pay at discharge

Even in a cashless claim, you pay the hospital for items the policy does not cover, such as non-medical consumables and registration or administrative charges. You also pay any co-payment or room-rent excess that applies under your policy schedule, and any amount above your remaining sum insured.

## Cashless vs reimbursement

The difference between cashless and reimbursement claims is who pays the hospital. In a cashless claim at a network hospital, we pay the hospital directly after pre-authorization. In a reimbursement claim, at any hospital, you pay first and then submit the claim form, original bills, discharge summary and diagnostic reports within 15 days of discharge. Reimbursement claims are processed within 7 to 10 working days of receiving complete documents.
