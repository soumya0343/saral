# How to File a Claim

For a cashless claim, get treated at a network hospital and present your health card at the
insurance desk; the hospital raises a pre-authorization request, which is decided within 4 hours.

For reimbursement claims, pay the hospital, then submit the claim form, original bills, discharge
summary, and diagnostic reports within 15 days of discharge. Claims are processed within 7 to 10
working days of receiving complete documents.

You can check the status of a filed claim at any time using your claim ID.
