# Health Insurance — Exclusions and Waiting Periods

A waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy start date, during which only accidental
hospitalization is covered. Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 36 months
of continuous coverage.

Specified illnesses such as cataract, hernia, and joint replacement have a waiting period of 24
months. Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless due to accident), and self-inflicted injuries
are permanently excluded. Treatment outside India is not covered under the base plan.
