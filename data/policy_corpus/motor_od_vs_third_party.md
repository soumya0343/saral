# Motor Insurance — Own Damage vs Third-Party

## What third-party motor cover is

Third-party liability cover pays for injury, death or property damage that your vehicle causes to other people. Third-party cover is mandatory by law for every vehicle driven on public roads in India. It does not pay for any damage to your own vehicle.

## What own-damage motor cover is

Own-damage cover pays for loss of or damage to your own insured vehicle from accident, fire, theft and natural calamities such as floods. Own-damage claims for theft and major accidents require an FIR. Depreciation on replaced parts is deducted unless you have the zero-depreciation add-on, and a compulsory deductible applies to each own-damage claim.

## Comprehensive vs standalone motor policies

A comprehensive motor policy combines own-damage and third-party liability cover, and includes personal accident cover for the owner-driver up to INR 15 lakh. A standalone own-damage policy can be bought only if you already hold a valid third-party policy. A third-party-only policy costs the least, but you bear all repair costs for your own vehicle.

## What own-damage cover does not include

Normal wear and tear, mechanical or electrical breakdown, damage while driving without a valid driving licence or under the influence of alcohol, and use of the vehicle outside its permitted purpose are not covered under own-damage cover. Consequential losses, such as engine damage from driving through water after a breakdown, are covered only with the engine protection add-on.
