# Network Hospitals

## What a network hospital is

A network hospital is a hospital that has an agreement with us to provide cashless treatment to our health policyholders. At a network hospital, the hospital raises a pre-authorization request and the approved bill is settled directly with us, so you do not need to arrange the covered amount yourself.

## How to find a network hospital near you

You can search the current list of network hospitals by city or pincode in your online account or app, or ask customer support to share network hospitals near you. The network changes from time to time as hospitals join or leave, so check that your chosen hospital is still in the network before a planned admission.

## Treatment at a non-network hospital

You can get treated at any registered hospital, including one outside our network. At a non-network hospital, cashless treatment is not available: you pay the bill and then file a reimbursement claim with the claim form, original bills, discharge summary and diagnostic reports within 15 days of discharge. The coverage and limits of your policy stay the same.

## Emergency admission at the nearest hospital

In a medical emergency, get treated at the nearest hospital first. If it is a network hospital, the hospital can request cashless approval within 24 hours of admission. If it is not a network hospital, keep all original bills and reports and file a reimbursement claim after discharge.
