# Downloading Policy Documents

## How to download your policy document

You can download your policy document, policy schedule, premium receipts and tax certificates at any time from your online account or app, after logging in with the OTP sent to your registered mobile number. The policy document is also emailed to your registered email address when the policy is issued and at every renewal.

## Getting a duplicate policy copy

If you lost your policy document or need a printed copy, raise a request through support. An electronic copy is sent to your registered email right away, and a duplicate physical copy can be couriered to your registered address. Policy documents are shared only with the verified policy owner.

## If you did not receive your policy documents

If your policy document did not arrive by email, check that your registered email address is correct and look in your spam folder. If the registered email is wrong, update it after identity verification, and the documents can then be sent again.
