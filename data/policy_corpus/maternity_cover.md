# Maternity Cover

## Is maternity covered in the base health plan

Maternity expenses, such as childbirth and the related hospital stay, are not covered under the base health plan. Maternity cover is available only when your policy includes the maternity add-on, which is shown in your policy schedule. You can check whether your policy has the maternity add-on in your schedule or by asking support.

## Maternity waiting period and limits

The maternity add-on has a waiting period of 24 months of continuous coverage from the date the add-on was first taken. Delivery expenses, whether normal or caesarean, are paid up to the maternity limit stated in your schedule, for a maximum of two deliveries during the life of the policy. Pre-natal and post-natal expenses are covered within the usual 30-day pre-hospitalization and 60-day post-hospitalization limits.

## Newborn baby cover

Under the maternity add-on, a newborn baby is covered from birth for up to 90 days within the mother's sum insured, including medically necessary treatment. To keep the baby covered after 90 days, add the baby to the policy as an insured member by raising a request within 90 days of birth.

## What maternity cover does not include

Infertility treatment, surrogacy-related expenses and voluntary termination of pregnancy within the first 12 weeks, unless medically necessary, are not covered even when you have the maternity add-on. Complications of pregnancy that need hospitalization are handled under the add-on's terms and limits, so check your policy schedule.
