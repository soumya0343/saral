# Health Insurance — Coverage

Our health insurance policy covers hospitalization expenses including room rent, ICU charges,
surgeon fees, diagnostic tests, and prescribed medicines during an admitted stay of 24 hours or
more. Pre-hospitalization expenses up to 30 days and post-hospitalization expenses up to 60 days
are reimbursable when related to the same illness.

Day-care procedures that do not require 24-hour admission (for example cataract surgery and
dialysis) are covered as listed in the policy schedule. Ambulance charges are reimbursed up to
INR 2,000 per hospitalization.

The sum assured is the maximum amount payable in a policy year across all claims.
