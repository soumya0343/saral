# Personal Loan — Eligibility and Documents

## Who is eligible for a personal loan

You can apply for a personal loan if you are between 21 and 60 years of age, are salaried or self-employed with a steady income, and have a minimum net monthly income of ₹25,000. A credit score of 700 or above improves your chances of approval and of a lower rate. Final eligibility depends on your income, existing EMIs and credit history.

## Personal loan amount, tenure and interest rate

A personal loan is unsecured, so no collateral or guarantor is needed. Loan amounts range from ₹50,000 to ₹25 lakh, repayable in EMIs over 12 to 60 months. Interest rates range from 10.5% to 24% per annum depending on your profile. Your total EMIs, including the new loan, should generally not exceed half of your net monthly income.

## Documents required for a personal loan

Keep ready your PAN card, an identity and address proof such as Aadhaar or passport, your last 3 months' salary slips (or the last 2 years' income tax returns if self-employed), and bank statements for the last 6 months. A photograph and a signed application form are also needed.

## Why a personal loan application may be declined

Common reasons for a declined personal loan application are a low credit score, recent missed payments, high existing EMIs compared to income, or incomplete documents. Improving your credit score and reducing existing debt before you reapply helps. Applying to many lenders in a short time can also lower your score, so wait a few months between applications.
