# Nominee Registration and Change

## What a nominee is

A nominee is the person you name to receive the policy benefit, such as the death claim amount under a term life policy, if something happens to you. Registering a nominee makes the claim process faster for your family, because the benefit can be paid directly to the named person after verification.

## Who can add or change a nominee

Only the verified policy owner can register or change a nominee. A nominee change is an account-changing action, so it needs identity verification, usually an OTP sent to your registered mobile number. Requests made on the policy owner's behalf by anyone else, including family members, are declined.

## How to add or change a nominee

Raise a nominee change request through support or your account with each nominee's full name, date of birth and relationship to you. You can name more than one nominee and split the benefit by percentage. A new nomination replaces the earlier one once it is recorded, and you receive a confirmation on your registered contact details.

## Nominee who is a minor

If a nominee is below 18 years of age, you must also name an appointee, an adult who receives the benefit on the minor's behalf until the nominee turns 18. You can change the nominee or appointee any number of times during the policy term.
