# Policy Renewal and Portability

## How to renew your policy

You can renew your policy from your online account or app, or by asking support for a payment link, using UPI, net banking, debit or credit card, or an auto-debit mandate. Renew on or before the due date to keep continuous cover. A grace period of 30 days is allowed after the due date for renewal.

## Why timely renewal matters for health policies

For a health policy, continuous renewal keeps your waiting-period credit, such as time served towards the 36-month pre-existing disease waiting period, and your no-claim bonus. If a health policy is not renewed in time and lapses, accrued waiting-period benefits are reset, and reinstatement may require fresh underwriting.

## Changes you can make at renewal

At renewal, you can increase your sum insured, add family members or add-ons, or change the plan variant. An increase in sum insured may need a fresh health declaration, and waiting periods apply afresh to the increased part of the cover. Health policies are renewable for life, and renewal is not refused because you made claims in earlier years.

## Porting your health policy to another insurer

Health insurance portability lets you move your policy from one insurer to another without losing waiting-period credit. Apply to the new insurer at least 45 days before your current policy's renewal date, with your current policy documents and claim history. Porting is allowed only at renewal, and the waiting period already served is credited up to the previous sum insured.
