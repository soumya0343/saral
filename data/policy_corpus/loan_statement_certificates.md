# Loan Statement, Interest Certificate and NOC

## Loan statement and amortization schedule

You can download your loan statement and amortization schedule from your account at any time. The loan statement shows every EMI paid, any charges, and the outstanding principal. The amortization schedule shows how each remaining EMI is split between principal and interest for the rest of the tenure.

## Loan interest certificate

A loan interest certificate shows the total principal and interest you paid during a financial year. It can be downloaded from your account after the financial year ends, or requested through support. Interest on a personal loan is generally not tax-deductible unless the money was used for an eligible purpose such as a business or a home purchase; please consult a tax adviser.

## No-objection certificate (NOC)

A no-objection certificate (NOC) confirms that your loan is fully repaid and nothing is due. The NOC is issued within 7 days of full repayment, whether the loan ends on schedule or is foreclosed. Keep the NOC safely and check your credit report after about 30 to 45 days to confirm that the loan shows as closed.

## Getting loan documents by email

Loan statements, certificates and the NOC can also be sent to your registered email address on request. They are sent only to the verified borrower's registered contact details. If your registered email has changed, update it after identity verification before requesting documents.
