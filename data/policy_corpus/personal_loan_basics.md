# Personal Loan — Basics

A personal loan is an unsecured loan disbursed based on income and credit score, repayable in
equated monthly installments (EMIs) over 12 to 60 months. Interest rates range from 10.5% to 24%
per annum depending on the applicant's profile.

Foreclosure is allowed after 6 EMIs, subject to a foreclosure charge of up to 4% of the
outstanding principal. Missing an EMI attracts a late-payment fee and may affect your credit
score.
