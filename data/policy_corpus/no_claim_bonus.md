# No-Claim Bonus in Health Insurance

## What the health no-claim bonus is

The health no-claim bonus, also called cumulative bonus, rewards you for a claim-free policy year. For every policy year with no claim, your sum insured increases by 10% of the base sum insured at renewal, at no extra premium, up to a maximum of 50% of the base sum insured.

## Example of no-claim bonus

If your base sum insured is ₹5,00,000 and you make no claim for two policy years in a row, your total cover becomes ₹6,00,000 from the third year. After five claim-free years, the cover reaches the maximum of ₹7,50,000, which is 50% more than the base sum insured, without any increase in premium for the bonus.

## What happens to the bonus after a claim

If a claim is paid in a policy year, the accumulated no-claim bonus is reduced by 10% of the base sum insured at the next renewal. The bonus never goes below zero, so your base sum insured is never reduced. A claim that is rejected does not reduce the bonus.

## Keeping your no-claim bonus

The health no-claim bonus is kept only if the policy is renewed within the 30-day grace period. If the policy lapses, the accumulated bonus is lost. For the car and two-wheeler no-claim bonus, which works as a premium discount, see the motor no-claim bonus FAQ.
