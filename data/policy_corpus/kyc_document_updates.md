# KYC and Document Updates

## What KYC is

KYC (Know Your Customer) is the process of verifying your identity and address. KYC is required when you buy a policy or take a loan, and before a claim or refund is paid. Accepted KYC documents include PAN card, Aadhaar with the first 8 digits masked, passport, voter ID and driving licence.

## Updating your address, name or other KYC details

To update your address, name, date of birth or PAN, raise a request through support or your account with a supporting document: for example, a new address proof for an address change, or a marriage certificate or gazette notification for a name change. KYC updates are made within 7 working days of receiving valid documents.

## Updating your mobile number or email

Your registered mobile number and email address can be updated by the policy owner after identity verification. A new mobile number takes effect only after it is verified, because it is used for OTP-based authentication and claim notifications. Until the new number is verified, OTPs and alerts continue to go to the old registered number.

## Who can request KYC changes

Only the policy owner or borrower can update their own KYC and contact details. Requests made on behalf of another person are declined, and customer data is shared only with the verified policyholder. Sensitive details, such as ID numbers, are masked in communications.
