# Complaint Escalation and Timelines

## Step 1: Raise a complaint with support

Raise your complaint with support by chat, phone or email, and you will receive a ticket ID. Complaints are acknowledged within 24 hours and resolved within 15 days. Keep your ticket ID to track the status of your complaint at any time.

## Step 2: Escalate to the Grievance Redressal Officer

If your complaint is not resolved within 15 days, or you are not satisfied with the response, escalate it in writing to the Grievance Redressal Officer, quoting your ticket ID. For a rejected or partly settled claim, you can appeal to the Grievance Redressal Officer within 15 days of the settlement intimation, quoting the claim number and the clause under which the deduction or rejection was made.

## Step 3: Approach the Insurance Ombudsman

If you are not satisfied with the Grievance Redressal Officer's reply, or you have not received a final reply within 30 days of your complaint, you can approach the Insurance Ombudsman. The complaint to the Ombudsman must be made within one year of the insurer's final reply, and the Ombudsman service is free for policyholders.

## Escalating a personal loan complaint

For personal loan complaints, the first two steps are the same: support, then the Grievance Redressal Officer. If a loan complaint is still unresolved after 30 days, or you are unhappy with the final reply, you may approach the banking ombudsman.
