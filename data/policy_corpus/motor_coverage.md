# Motor Insurance — Coverage

The comprehensive motor policy covers own-damage to the insured vehicle from accident, fire,
theft, and natural calamities, plus third-party liability for injury or property damage. Personal
accident cover for the owner-driver is included up to INR 15 lakh.

Add-ons such as zero-depreciation, engine protection, and roadside assistance are available at
extra premium. Claims for own-damage require an FIR for theft and major accidents.
