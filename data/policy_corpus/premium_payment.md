# Premium Payment and Renewal

Premiums can be paid annually, half-yearly, or monthly via UPI, net banking, debit/credit card,
or auto-debit mandate. A grace period of 30 days is allowed after the due date for renewal; cover
continues during the grace period for life products but a lapse in health policies resets accrued
waiting-period benefits if not renewed in time.

A policy marked "lapsed" must be reinstated, which may require fresh underwriting.
