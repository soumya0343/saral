# Riders — Critical Illness and Accidental Death

## What a rider is

A rider is an optional add-on to a base policy that gives extra cover for an extra premium. Riders are usually added when you buy the policy or at renewal. A rider's terms, including its list of covered conditions and waiting periods, are printed in your policy schedule, and a rider claim is paid only when those specific conditions are met.

## Critical illness rider

A critical illness rider pays a lump sum on the first diagnosis of a critical illness named in the rider's list, such as cancer of specified stage, first heart attack, stroke, kidney failure or major organ transplant. Only the illnesses listed in your rider schedule are covered; a condition that is not on the list is not payable under the rider, however serious it is.

## Critical illness waiting and survival periods

A critical illness rider has an initial waiting period, typically 90 days from the rider start date, and a survival period, typically 30 days after diagnosis, which the insured person must survive for the benefit to be paid. The lump sum does not depend on actual hospital bills and is separate from hospitalization claims under the base policy.

## Accidental death benefit rider

An accidental death benefit rider pays an additional sum assured, over and above the base sum assured, if the insured person dies due to an accident and death occurs within 180 days of the accident. Death from self-inflicted injury, intoxication, or hazardous activities excluded in the schedule is not covered under this rider.
