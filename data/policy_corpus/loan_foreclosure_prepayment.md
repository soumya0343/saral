# Loan Foreclosure and Part-Prepayment

## What loan foreclosure means

Foreclosure means repaying your entire outstanding personal loan in one payment before the tenure ends. Foreclosure is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid. A foreclosure charge of up to 4% of the outstanding principal applies, plus applicable taxes. Closing the loan early saves the interest you would have paid over the remaining tenure.

## How to foreclose your personal loan

Raise a foreclosure request through support or your account to get a foreclosure statement showing the outstanding principal, interest due up to the payment date, and the foreclosure charge. Pay the total by the date shown on the statement. After full repayment, a no-objection certificate (NOC) is issued within 7 days, and the loan is reported as closed to credit bureaus.

## Part-prepayment of a personal loan

Part-prepayment means paying a lump sum towards the principal without closing the loan. It is allowed after 6 EMIs have been paid, up to 25% of the outstanding principal in a financial year, with a charge of up to 2% of the amount prepaid. After a part-prepayment, you can choose to reduce your EMI or keep the EMI the same and shorten the tenure.

## Choosing between foreclosure and part-prepayment

Choose foreclosure to become debt-free and get your NOC, or part-prepayment to reduce interest while keeping the loan running. Both options are available only after the first 6 EMIs have been paid. Missing an EMI before foreclosing can add late-payment fees to the amount due.
