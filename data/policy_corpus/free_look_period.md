# Free-Look Period

## What the free-look period is

The free-look period is the time after you receive a new policy document during which you can review its terms and cancel the policy if you are not satisfied, without giving any reason. The free-look period is 30 days from the date you receive the policy document, whether you bought the policy online or offline.

## How to cancel a policy in the free-look period

To cancel under free-look, raise a cancellation request through support or your account within 30 days of receiving the policy document, quoting the policy number and your bank details for the refund. Only the verified policy owner can request a free-look cancellation.

## How much refund you get on free-look cancellation

The free-look refund is the premium you paid minus a proportionate charge for the days you were covered, any medical examination costs we paid, and stamp duty. The refund is credited within 15 days of receiving the cancellation request, to the bank account of the policy owner. Once the policy is cancelled under free-look, the cover ends from that date.

## When free-look does not apply

Free-look applies only to a newly bought policy, not to renewals. If a claim has been made during the free-look period, the policy cannot be cancelled under free-look. After the free-look period ends, you can still cancel the policy, but the refund then follows the cancellation terms in your policy document.
