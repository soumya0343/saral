# Term Life Insurance — Coverage

## What term life insurance covers

A term life policy is a pure protection plan. If the insured person dies during the policy term while the policy is in force, the sum assured is paid to the nominee. The policy must be in force, meaning premiums are paid up to date or the death happens within the 30-day grace period, for the death claim to be payable.

## Is there a maturity benefit in term life

A pure term life plan has no maturity or survival benefit. If the insured person survives the full policy term, no amount is paid and the cover simply ends. This is why term life premiums are much lower than savings plans for the same sum assured. The premium stays the same for the whole term once the policy is issued.

## Suicide clause and other exclusions in term life

If death is due to suicide within 12 months of the policy start date, only the premiums paid are refunded, not the sum assured. Death while the policy is lapsed is not covered. Not disclosing material facts at proposal, such as an existing illness or tobacco use, can lead to rejection of the death claim.

## Adding riders to term life

You can add riders such as a critical illness rider or an accidental death benefit rider to a term life policy for an extra premium. A rider pays in addition to the base sum assured when its own conditions are met.
