# Tax Benefits on Insurance Premiums

## Tax benefit on life insurance premiums

Premiums paid for life insurance, including term life, can be claimed as a deduction under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act, within the overall Section 80C limit of ₹1.5 lakh per financial year. The death benefit paid to the nominee is generally tax-free under Section 10(10D). The Section 80C and 80D deductions are available under the old tax regime.

## Tax benefit on health insurance premiums

Health insurance premiums can be claimed under Section 80D: up to ₹25,000 a year for yourself, your spouse and children (₹50,000 if you are a senior citizen), plus an additional ₹25,000 for parents (₹50,000 if they are senior citizens). Preventive health check-ups up to ₹5,000 are included within these limits. The premium must be paid by a non-cash mode to qualify.

## Getting your premium certificate for tax

A premium paid certificate for tax filing can be downloaded from your account or requested through support, and it is sent to your registered email. Tax rules change from time to time and depend on your situation, so please consult a tax adviser before filing.
