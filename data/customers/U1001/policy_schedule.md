# Arogya Secure — Health Insurance Policy Schedule

**Policyholder:** Asha Verma
**Policy No.:** POL1001
**Plan:** Arogya Secure Individual (Variant B — with Room-Rent Cap)
**Sum Insured:** ₹5,00,000
**Policy Period:** 01-Nov-2025 to 31-Oct-2026
**Annual Premium:** ₹14,500

## 1. Coverage

1.1 Hospitalisation expenses (in-patient) are covered up to the Sum Insured for medically
necessary treatment of an Illness or Injury contracted during the Policy Period.

1.2 Pre-hospitalisation expenses are covered for 30 days and post-hospitalisation expenses
for 60 days from the date of discharge.

## 2. Waiting Periods

2.1 An initial waiting period of 30 days applies from the policy start date, except for
hospitalisation arising from an Accident.

2.3 Pre-Existing Diseases (PED) declared at proposal are subject to a waiting period of
36 months of continuous coverage before any claim relating to them is admissible.

## 3. Room Rent

3.1 This is a Variant B policy: room rent is capped at 1% of the Sum Insured per day
(₹5,000/day) for a Single Private AC Room. ICU rent is capped at 2% per day.

## 4. Claim Settlement Conditions (load-bearing for adjudication)

4.1 **Proportionate Deduction.** Where the room rent actually incurred exceeds the eligible
room-rent cap in Clause 3.1, all associated variable charges (surgeon fees, OT, consultant
visits) are settled in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to the actual
room rent. This is the proportionate-deduction clause.

4.2 **Co-payment.** A mandatory co-payment of 10% of every admissible claim amount is borne
by the policyholder under this variant.

4.3 Non-medical consumables (gloves, PPE, administrative charges) listed in Annexure-I of the
IRDAI consumables list are not payable.

## 5. Exclusions

5.1 Cosmetic surgery, dental treatment (unless arising from Accident), and infertility
treatment are excluded.

5.2 Treatment taken outside India is excluded under this variant.

## 6. Grievance & Appeal

6.1 A partially settled or rejected claim may be appealed in writing to the Grievance
Redressal Officer within 15 days of the settlement intimation, quoting the claim number and
the clause under which the deduction was made.
