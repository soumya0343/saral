## Assessor's reasoning

The patient was admitted in a Deluxe Room at ₹9,000/day. The policy is **Variant B with a
room-rent cap of ₹5,000/day (Clause 3.1)**. Because actual room rent (₹9,000) exceeds the
eligible cap (₹5,000), **proportionate deduction under Clause 4.1** applies: associated
variable charges were settled at the ratio 5,000 / 9,000 = 0.556.

Breakdown:
- Eligible charges after proportionate deduction (Clause 4.1): ₹38,000
- Less non-payable consumables (Clause 4.3): ₹2,000
- Net admissible: ₹36,000
- Less 10% co-payment (Clause 4.2): ₹3,600
- **Net payable: ₹32,400**

## Why the deduction happened (customer-facing explanation)

Your claim was not rejected — it was **partially settled at ₹32,400**. The reduction is for
two reasons specific to *your* policy variant: (1) you were admitted in a room costing more
than your ₹5,000/day cap, which triggers **proportionate deduction under Clause 4.1**, and
(2) your variant carries a **mandatory 10% co-payment under Clause 4.2**. If you upgrade to a
no-cap variant at renewal, Clause 4.1 would no longer apply.

Appeal window: 15 days from intimation, per Clause 6.1.
